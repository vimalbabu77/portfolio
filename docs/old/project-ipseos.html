Overview

IPSEOS is a premier European satellite internet operator and service provider based in Brussels, Belgium. With over 30 years of industry expertise, the company specializes in delivering high-speed, secure, and independent connectivity solutions worldwide.

The site showcases a comprehensive portfolio, including GEO and LEO satellite services, maritime internet, and portable communication systems for remote areas. Catering to diverse sectors like mining, NGOs, and maritime, IPSEOS emphasizes data sovereignty and reliability by routing traffic through its private teleport infrastructure. Their platform serves as a vital hub for professional-grade VSAT solutions, ensuring stable communications even in the most challenging geopolitical environments.
